Research

LLM agents for transportation policy making

Transit plans that look optimal in a model often stall once the public weighs in. I use LLM agents, grounded in neighborhood demographics, to simulate how communities would vote on a policy, and compare those collective preferences with the outcomes a calibrated transit design model predicts. Current work closes the loop: agents rank candidate policies, a preference model learns from their rankings, and the search proposes the next slate, across thousands of feasible Chicago transit-finance policies.

Related: TR Part C 2026, INFORMS 2026

Transit system design and operations

I formulate optimization models for how a city should split resources between fixed-route transit and paratransit, including services run with autonomous pods, and stress-test the resulting fleet plans for Chicago against vehicle capacity and multi-period demand.

Related: TRB 2025 presentation, TR Part D 2025

Human mobility and travel behavior from data

From millions of GPS records I reconstruct activity chains, infer travel modes and trip purposes, and classify traveler types. Earlier work studied Citi Bike riders in New York City, built a fairness-aware spatiotemporal network for Chicago travel-demand forecasting, and contributed to a city-scale CO2 emission monitoring platform.

Related: Travel Behaviour and Society 2024, Applied Energy 2023
